D1 - HISTORICAL MOVEMENT FLAGS: how large is this month by past standards?
==============================================================================
  PURPOSE: to say how unusual a month is BY THE HISTORICAL STANDARD OF THIS
  SERIES. Nothing more.

  THESE ARE DESCRIPTIVE FLAGS, NOT BUSINESS ACTION THRESHOLDS:
    ELEVATED = above the p90 of historical absolute monthly moves
    EXTREME  = above the p95

  A flag says a move is large relative to this series past. It does NOT
  say a business should act, reprice or renegotiate. Whether a move
  matters depends on that company cost exposure, its margin and its
  ability to pass cost through - none of which is in this dataset. Turning
  a flag into a trigger requires those three inputs first.

  METHOD: distribution of each jurisdiction own month-over-month %
  change, pooled across the 37 jurisdictions and the 14 month-pairs in the
  primary window. Signed and absolute percentiles are both reported: a cost

D2 - DIESEL SELF-GENERATION AGAINST A FIXED JULY 2025 REFERENCE TARIFF
==============================================================================
  QUESTION: how does the fuel cost of self-generation compare with a FIXED
  GRID REFERENCE TARIFF, and how has that comparison moved as diesel rose?

  THE TWO SIDES DO NOT COVER THE SAME PERIOD. Diesel runs monthly to
  2026-05. The NERC tariff is a SINGLE JULY 2025 CROSS-SECTION and is held
  FIXED throughout. This is therefore a comparison against a REFERENCE
  BENCHMARK, not a like-for-like comparison of two current prices. If the
  tariff has risen since July 2025, the true multiples are smaller than
  those shown. This corpus cannot say whether it has.

  ASSUMPTION 1 - GENERATOR EFFICIENCY (assumed, not measured): a diesel
  generator is assumed to deliver 3.0-3.5 kWh per litre at load. The range
  2.5-4.0 is carried through so the result can be read at any efficiency.
  A business MUST substitute its own measured burn per hour divided by
  load before using this.

  ASSUMPTION 2 - REFERENCE TARIFF (fixed benchmark, July 2025): the grid
  side is the NERC Band A tariff as published in the July 2025 order. It is
  held constant across all months shown.

  Neither side includes the generator itself, servicing, oil or downtime,
  nor grid connection charges, fixed charges or outage losses.

  GRAIN WARNING: diesel is jurisdiction-grain, NERC tariffs are DisCo-grain
  and DisCos are not jurisdictions. This is NOT a geographic join. It
  compares a cross-jurisdiction median diesel cost against a national band
  benchmark. Band A is used as the reference precisely because it is the
  one band with ZERO spread across all 11 DisCos (a01 F8), so no geographic
  assignment is needed for it.

D3 - DID CPI TRACK OBSERVED TRANSPORT-FARE GROWTH?
==============================================================================
  QUESTION: transport allowances are commonly uprated by headline inflation.
  Over the measured months, did CPI growth track observed fare growth?

  WHAT THIS MEASURES: the gap between two published growth rates. A
  positive gap means an allowance indexed ONLY to CPI would have LAGGED
  observed fare growth, reducing the commuting purchasing power that
  allowance provides. It is NOT a statement about total real pay, which
  depends on salary, other allowances and actual commuting patterns that
  this dataset does not contain.

  METHOD: per jurisdiction, year-on-year growth in okada and intracity bus
  fares against year-on-year CPI all-items, in the months where BOTH exist.

  months usable (CPI too)   : 4
  fare months with NO CPI   : ['2026-02-01']  <- the 2026-02 CPI YoY hole (a01 S4). Excluded, not imputed.
