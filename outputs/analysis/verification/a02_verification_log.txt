==============================================================================
  SCOPE OF THIS TEST. Every published rank is a VALID SNAPSHOT of its month:
  the values are correctly extracted and correctly ordered, and nothing here
  questions them. The question is narrower and practical - will a rank taken
  this month still describe the same jurisdiction next month, well enough to
  anchor a PERSISTENT location decision such as siting or a standing supplier
  preference?

  For each metric: the typical monthly price move, against the cross-
  jurisdiction dispersion it has to traverse. churn = mean absolute change in
  a jurisdiction's rank from one month to the next, out of 37 positions.

  The 1.0 cut is a PROJECT DECISION-USE HEURISTIC adopted for consistency,
  not a statistical standard and not a data-quality verdict.
